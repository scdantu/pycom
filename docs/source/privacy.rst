Privacy Notice
===============
Dear Users,

When you access the PyCoM website or the PyCoM API, in the log files secured on Brunel servers, we record your IP address and collect information on components of the database you have accessed. 

We process these statistics and delete the IP address. These statistics help us refine the PyCoM software/database and help us apply for and secure grants to sustain this service.

**We do not collect your email ID or any other personal identifying information about you.**

If you have any concerns about how this data is being used please contact the PyCoM team at pycom@brunel.ac.uk or the Brunel Data Protection Team at data-protection@brunel.ac.uk.

For more detailed information on Brunel University's General Privacy notice please refer to the following `website <https://www.brunel.ac.uk/About-this-website/Privacy-Policy-and-Copyright-Statement>`_.
